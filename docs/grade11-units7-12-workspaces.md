# Grade 11 Units 7–12 workspaces

The supplied Grade 11 PDFs are the curriculum source. Original reading passages, grammar explanations, diagrams, underlining, dialogue models, writing samples and starters remain available as local textbook scans. The scans retain native source image resolution. Shared pages are cropped at skill boundaries for the lesson view; opening an image shows the complete original page.

Verified task transcriptions accompany these scans. Instructions and pre-reading/comprehension questions have Burmese translations; the source passages and remaining prompts are not presented as fully translated text. English structure cards and Burmese structure explanations are separate. All 61 full-sentence reading prompts use reviewed phrase boundaries rather than the shared heuristic parser. No new story, dialogue, essay or personal sample response was authored.

The six skills are separate in both direct unit routes and the curriculum explorer. Drafts are stored per unit and skill in session storage, with an in-memory fallback. Drafts save on input, and fields wait for restoration before becoming editable. Progress counts actual response fields and excludes printed worked examples. Personal questions, sentence rewrites, compositions and audio-dependent tasks have no single-answer correctness test. Closed, source-derived answers are stored separately from the prompts and are not an official answer key.

Listening audio was not supplied. The workspaces show one notice per Listening view, accept learner notes/responses, and do not substitute Grade 10 recordings or invent listening answers. Grammar video placeholders remain visible for later links. Pre-reading has no Translate Instructions button. The removed answer disclaimer is not rendered.

Special controls include multi-part story choices, matching tables, compound-word responses, disaster categories, TRUE/FALSE and TRUE/FALSE/NOT MENTIONED listening choices, and composition word counts for the printed 100-, 200-, and 200–250-word tasks. Unit 11's emphasis exercise explicitly points learners to the original underlined source.

Grade 10 files, existing Grade 11 Units 1–6 data, AI workflow/providers/routes, shared exercise controls, authentication, database, dependencies and build configuration are unchanged. No paid AI calls, authenticated database writes or production build are part of verification.
